# Research summary (Oct 2026) — applied, not exhaustive

Sources searched: press (Diario Libre, La Razón MX, Merca2.0), retailer pages (Walmart MX, Primark, Mercado Libre, Falabella),
agency write-ups of Meta video best practice, ad-intelligence pages for Eberjey. No verified sales/ROAS data was found for any
creative: everything below is a documented pattern or a labelled hypothesis, never "this ad sells more".

## Documented
- Matching Christmas pajamas remain a promoted category in MX (Walmart MX, Mercado Libre, Amazon) and are tied to
  family photos for social media (Diario Libre) and to "a new family tradition" framing (Primark 2025).
- Buyers are told to check size charts per family member and to order early for delivery before Christmas
  (Diario Libre, La Razón, Falabella) → sizing/fit and timing are real purchase anxieties.
- Mass-market messaging is price-led (≈ $300 MXN sets, up to 50 % off — Merca2.0) and print-led (reindeer, snowflake, plaid).
- Premium sleepwear (Eberjey et al.) leans on hero sets, tactile close-ups, soft-light lifestyle, "pajamas for every hour"
  (sleep/lounge blur — Sourcy FW25-26 report).
- Meta video guidance (secondhand, agency-cited): value is front-loaded in the first 3 s; open on motion/face/situation,
  never on a logo; design for sound-on but caption key phrases; keep text in the central safe area.

## Hypotheses for RE:SET (to test, not proven)
- Saturated: "pijamas para toda la familia" + price; generic "la mejor pijama". Avoid as hooks.
- Differentiators visible in the material: a real person talking (trust vs. catalog/price ads), print detail close-ups,
  basics that look good enough to wear all day, and the matching drawstring bag that turns into a travel case.
- Sizes, fabric composition, prices, promos and stock are NOT verified → not claimed in any ad.
